Publish & transfer

Your edits are saved as drafts in this browser only — a co-editor on another laptop won't see them yet. To make them live for every reader, click Download to publish and commit the downloaded published-content.js to the site (readers then see it, and refresh automatically the next time you publish). To hand drafts to a co-editor without publishing, download the bundle and have them load it.
